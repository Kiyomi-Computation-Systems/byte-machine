# 0039: Cross-cell generation equality is proved transitively, through committed goldens

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: accepted
Milestone: 9, Gate 0 (milestone-9-design.md §4.2)

## Context

Decided against `main` at `c8e5fb6`; `src/` byte-identical to `62671b2`.

`§24.2` stage 7 ("compare generated output across every Tier 1 OS/architecture cell") and `§22:1314`
read literally as a cross-job artefact comparison: every cell uploads its generated bytes, a final
job downloads and diffs them. That mechanism needs artefact storage, a fan-in job, and a failure
mode ("cell 3's upload is missing") that has nothing to do with the generator.

The tree already implements the cheaper, stronger thing. `determinism-one-byte-tests.sps`'s header
states the argument and **names this milestone as its completion**: byte-identity within a process,
*plus* equality to a committed golden that every cell and lane compares against, gives
cross-process, cross-lane and cross-cell identity transitively — the golden is the fixed point. Five
machines are covered today, their goldens in `test/fixtures/` (verified at `c8e5fb6`:
`generated-one-byte`, `-pause`, `-http-version`, `-accumulate`, `-counted`), and `.gitattributes:19`
marks `test/fixtures/** -text` so no checkout filter can touch the bytes.

The gap is the HTTP fixture — the largest machine in the tree, exercising more of the emitter than
the other five combined — whose golden Milestone 8 Slice ⑤ deferred **to this milestone by name** in
`determinism-http-tests.sps`'s header: "the committed-golden-file variant is DEFERRED … named for a
hardening slice or M9".

## Decision

**1. No cross-job comparison step is built.** `§24.2` stage 7 is satisfied by every cell that runs
executing the determinism lane pairs against the same committed bytes. This is a disposition of the
spec's *mechanism*, not of its requirement, and the ledger's `§26:1677` row says so in those words —
recorded as a disposition (obligation 6), not a rewording of the spec.

**2. Slice ① adds the sixth golden** — the HTTP fixture's generated bytes under `test/fixtures/`,
compared byte-for-byte by `determinism-http-tests.sps` exactly as the other five are compared.
Produced by the shipped emitter, compared by the shipped lane; byte-identity across two emissions
already holds (Milestone 8 Slice ⑤), and the golden adds the cross-process and cross-lane leg. This
is obligation 2.

**3. The cross-*cell* leg is exactly as wide as the cells that run.** The mechanism is total; its
coverage is not. With no Tier 1 CI this milestone (decision [0041]), the goldens are compared on
**macOS arm64 (local) only**. The ledger's `§26:1677` row records *both* — "mechanism complete;
compared in every cell that runs, one this milestone" — so a reader sees a total mechanism and a
one-cell coverage, never a silent partial. *Rejected, with the failure it would cause*: recording
`§26:1677` as `met` unqualified would claim cross-cell coverage the milestone did not buy — the
"silent waiver" `§7.3:203` forbids, arriving as an over-claimed ledger row.

## Consequences

- **Handed to Slice ①**: the sixth determinism golden and its lane pair, built by the shipped
  emitter and compared by the shipped lane.
- **Handed to Slice ⓪ / the ledger**: `§26:1677` written as "mechanism complete; one cell (local)
  this milestone, the rest deferred with decision [0041]".
- Risk accepted: a golden that drifts stale is one nobody regenerates — mitigated by the lane
  comparing it on **every run in every cell that runs**, which is the opposite of stale.
